# Research Specification

## Primary Research Question

How well do microplastic characteristics observed in food overlap with those investigated in human-health toxicity studies?

## Core Concept

Connect:

```text
Occurrence
→ Particle Characteristics
→ Exposure
→ Toxicological Evidence
```

using an ontology-driven knowledge graph.

## Initial Matching Dimensions

1. Polymer
2. Particle size
3. Shape
4. Exposure route

## Candidate Core Concepts

The ontology may eventually include concepts such as:

- Study
- Food
- Occurrence Observation
- Particle Profile
- Polymer
- Particle Size
- Shape
- Analytical Method
- Exposure Estimate
- Toxicity Experiment
- Biological System
- Exposure Route
- Dose
- Biological Endpoint
- Effect Measure

These are provisional concepts only.

Do not formalize the ontology until the source datasets have been inspected.

## Primary Outputs

- Ontology
- RDF knowledge graph
- SPARQL competency questions
- Evidence coverage analysis
- Evidence-gap visualization

## Initial Scope

Foodborne human exposure only.

Not included in V0.1:

- aquatic ecotoxicity
- AOP integration
- quantitative risk scoring
- unpublished or proprietary research data

## Provenance Principle

Every occurrence observation and toxicity record should eventually remain traceable to its original study or public data source.

## Current Dependency

The detailed occurrence-data schema will be finalized after the original food microplastic dataset is retrieved.

The ontology will therefore remain intentionally minimal until the source-data structure is verified.
